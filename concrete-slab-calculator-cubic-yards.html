Concrete Slab Calculator in Cubic Yards

Need to know how many cubic yards of concrete your slab requires? This guide explains the simple calculation and shows how to convert slab volume from cubic feet to cubic yards.

Quick Example: 10x10 Slab

Slab Size 10 × 10 ft
Thickness 4 inches
Concrete 1.23 yd³

What Is a Cubic Yard?

A cubic yard is a unit of volume commonly used when ordering ready-mix concrete in the United States. One cubic yard contains 27 cubic feet.

1 Cubic Yard = 27 Cubic Feet

Because slab dimensions are often measured in feet and inches, it is common to calculate the volume in cubic feet first and then convert the result into cubic yards.

How to Calculate Cubic Yards of Concrete

First calculate the slab volume in cubic feet:

Length × Width × Thickness = Cubic Feet

If thickness is given in inches, divide it by 12 to convert it to feet. Then divide the cubic-foot result by 27.

Cubic Feet ÷ 27 = Cubic Yards

Example: 10x10 Slab at 4 Inches Thick

Step 1: Convert thickness

Four inches converted to feet:

4 ÷ 12 = 0.333 feet

Step 2: Calculate cubic feet

Multiply length, width, and thickness:

10 × 10 × 0.333 = 33.33 cubic feet

Step 3: Convert to cubic yards

33.33 ÷ 27 = 1.23 cubic yards

The theoretical volume is therefore approximately 1.23 cubic yards.

Concrete Cubic Yard Examples

Here are some example calculations for a 10x10 foot slab:

  • 3 inches thick: about 0.93 cubic yards
  • 4 inches thick: about 1.23 cubic yards
  • 5 inches thick: about 1.54 cubic yards
  • 6 inches thick: about 1.85 cubic yards

These are calculated volumes before considering any additional quantity that may be appropriate for actual project conditions.

Important: Actual concrete requirements can vary because of uneven ground, measurement differences, formwork, spillage, and changes in slab thickness.

Why Use Cubic Yards?

Contractors and ready-mix suppliers in many parts of the United States commonly use cubic yards when discussing concrete quantities. Converting your slab volume into cubic yards makes it easier to compare your calculation with a supplier's quoted quantity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate cubic yards of concrete for a slab?

Multiply the slab length by width and thickness to get cubic feet. Then divide the cubic-foot result by 27 to get cubic yards.

How many cubic feet are in one cubic yard?

One cubic yard contains exactly 27 cubic feet.

How many cubic yards does a 10x10 slab need?

A 10x10 foot slab that is 4 inches thick requires approximately 1.23 cubic yards of concrete before any additional allowance.
