How to calculate concrete for a footing

For a simple rectangular footing, convert length, width, and depth to the same unit, then multiply the three dimensions. If the result is in cubic feet, divide by 27 to convert it to cubic yards. For several identical footings, calculate one footing and multiply by the number of footings.

Footing example

Suppose a rectangular footing is 10 feet long, 2 feet wide, and 1 foot deep. The volume is 10 × 2 × 1 = 20 cubic feet. Dividing by 27 gives about 0.74 cubic yards before any allowance.

Important project considerations

Footings are structural components. Required dimensions depend on soil, loads, frost depth, foundation design, reinforcement, local requirements, and site conditions. A volume calculator can estimate material quantity, but it should not be used to choose structural footing dimensions.

Ordering concrete

After calculating the theoretical volume, discuss the quantity and appropriate overage with the concrete supplier or project professional. For multiple footings or irregular foundation layouts, break the project into measurable sections and keep the units consistent.
