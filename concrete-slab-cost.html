Concrete Slab Cost Guide

Learn how to estimate concrete slab material cost using cubic yards, price per yard, waste allowance, delivery, labor, base, and reinforcement.

Start with concrete volume

The first step in a concrete slab cost estimate is calculating the required volume. Multiply length × width × thickness to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 for cubic yards. Add a reasonable planning allowance before using a supplier's price per cubic yard.

Material-only estimate

SlabCalc can multiply your recommended cubic yards by the price per cubic yard you enter. For example, if your calculated order quantity is 3 cubic yards and a supplier quotes $160 per cubic yard, the concrete material portion is approximately $480 before other project costs.

Other costs to consider

  • Concrete delivery or short-load fees
  • Pumping or placement equipment
  • Labor and finishing
  • Forms and edge materials
  • Gravel or specified base material
  • Rebar or wire mesh
  • Excavation, grading, and site preparation
  • Taxes and local supplier charges

Why quotes vary

Concrete prices vary by supplier, location, mix, order size, delivery distance, scheduling, and project requirements. Treat online calculations as planning estimates and request a current quote for the actual job.
