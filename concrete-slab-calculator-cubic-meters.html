Concrete Slab Calculator in Cubic Meters

Need to know how much concrete you need for a slab? This guide explains how to calculate concrete volume in cubic meters using a simple length × width × thickness formula.

How to Calculate Concrete in Cubic Meters

To calculate the amount of concrete required for a slab, measure the length, width, and thickness of the slab. Convert all measurements to meters and multiply them together.

Concrete Volume = Length × Width × Thickness

Result = cubic meters (m³)

Example: Concrete for a Slab

Example measurements

Length = 5 meters

Width = 4 meters

Thickness = 0.10 meters (100 mm)

Concrete volume = 5 × 4 × 0.10

2 cubic meters of concrete

Why Thickness Matters

Slab thickness has a direct effect on the amount of concrete required. A thicker slab requires more concrete even when the length and width stay the same.

For example, increasing a slab from 100 mm to 150 mm thick increases the concrete volume by 50%, assuming the length and width remain unchanged.

Convert Measurements to Meters First

When using cubic meters, make sure all measurements are in meters before calculating the volume.

  • 100 mm = 0.10 meters
  • 150 mm = 0.15 meters
  • 200 mm = 0.20 meters
  • 1 centimeter = 0.01 meters

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate concrete in cubic meters?

Multiply the length by the width and thickness, with all measurements converted to meters first. The result is the required volume in cubic meters.

What is the formula for concrete volume?

The basic formula is length × width × thickness. All three dimensions should use the same unit.

Should I order extra concrete?

It can be useful to allow a small amount of extra concrete for measurement differences, waste, and uneven ground conditions.
