ABOUT THE TOOL

About Concrete Slab Calculator

Concrete Slab Calculator is a free online tool designed to help homeowners, DIY builders, contractors, and construction professionals estimate the amount of concrete needed for slab projects.

What We Do

Our calculator makes concrete quantity estimates easier by converting slab dimensions into cubic feet and cubic yards.

Users can also add a waste allowance and enter their own local concrete price to estimate a project cost.

Who Is It For?

The tool can be useful for a wide range of users, including:

  • Homeowners planning concrete projects
  • DIY builders
  • Contractors and construction crews
  • Handymen and remodelers
  • People comparing concrete material estimates

How The Calculator Works

Enter the length, width, and thickness of your slab. The calculator converts the measurements into feet and calculates the concrete volume.

The basic calculation uses:

Length × Width × Thickness = Cubic Feet

Cubic feet are then converted to cubic yards by dividing by 27. A selected waste allowance can then be added to provide a recommended order quantity.

Why Waste Allowance Matters

Real construction projects can have small material losses caused by uneven surfaces, measuring differences, spills, or changes during installation.

Adding a waste allowance can help create a more practical ordering estimate.

Use Local Pricing

Concrete prices can vary by location, supplier, delivery fees, concrete mix, and project requirements.

That's why the calculator allows you to enter your own price per cubic yard instead of assuming one fixed national price.
