Concrete Bag Calculator

Estimate how many 40 lb, 60 lb, or 80 lb concrete bags you may need from your project volume and the manufacturer's stated yield.

How concrete bag estimates work

Bag quantity is calculated by dividing the concrete volume you need by the yield printed on the product label. Because different manufacturers and mixes have different yields, there is no universal number of cubic feet for every 40 lb, 60 lb, or 80 lb bag.

Example method

If a product label says one bag yields 0.60 cubic feet and your planned volume is 12 cubic feet, divide 12 by 0.60 to get 20 bags. Round up to a whole bag and consider the practical allowance recommended for your project.

Why the label matters

Bag size alone does not guarantee a specific yield. Always use the manufacturer's stated yield for the exact product. Follow the product instructions for water, mixing, placement, curing, and safety.

Ready-mix versus bags

Small projects may be practical with bagged concrete, while larger pours are often ordered from a ready-mix supplier by cubic yards. Compare the total material, delivery, equipment, labor, and handling requirements before deciding how to source the concrete.
